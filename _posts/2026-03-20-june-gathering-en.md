The spring 2026 St. Lawrence Regional Gathering will take place in Gananoque, Ontario, from June 12 to 14 2026. The gathering is for Friends attending Meetings between Peterborough, ON, and Montreal, QC (including Quebec City), but is open to all who can attend.

## Registration

Please fill out this [registration form](https://forms.gle/YzYGauay1N1XoC9H8){: rel="noopener" target="_blank"}

In May, we will be in touch with those who have registered with instructions for how to get to the gathering.

## Cost

The gathering is free but we encourage Friends to consider a donation to Ottawa Monthly Meeting, whose budget supports the gathering.

## Food

Please note that we ask Friends to bring a meal which can be shared potluck style.

We regret that we cannot provide food or coordinate the potluck this time so we ask Friends with dietary restrictions to expect that the potluck meals will not be to their needs.

Trips to the grocery store can occur throughout the gathering for those without a car.

Please [contact us](/contact) with any concerns or questions. We can also share information about rideshares.
